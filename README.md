# Project Remnants: Safehouse Expanded
## Unofficial Submod developed by iGalaxied

**Safehouse Expanded** is a passion project aiming to make safehouse management in Project Remnants more interesting, complex, and varied. The goal is to expand upon Project Remnants' existing safehouse systems, giving players more control over their community while introducing new mechanics and other quality of life changes.

Safehouse Expanded is designed to be modular. Each major feature is developed independently of eachother (with some level of integration between modules, when applicable) and packaed into a single addon, with modules being toggleable and configurable through your saves Sandbox Options.

__Currently, Safehouse Expanded includes the Advanced Scheduler module, with more features planned for future updates.__

## Current Modules
### Advanced Scheduler
Rather than having to manually assign survivors different jobs throughout the day, Advanced Scheduler introduces a Rimworld-inspired scheduler that allows players to organize their safehouses' daily routines.

Survivors can be assigned different shifts throughout the day, letting you set up guard shifts, breaks, and more. Now your people don't just stand around doing the same task all day-- your cook might make meals three times a day, but inbetween they can guard or haul.

**Features:**
- 24-hour scheduling: Assign a different activity for each hour of the day
- Individual schedules: Each safehouse resident has their own routine, work shifts, and designated sleep times.
- Jobs: Schedule any safehouse job, including guarding, hauling, farming, cooking, logging, and more.
- New sleep job: Added sleep jobs, allowing you to give your NPCs dedicated sleep times.
- Persistent schedules: Schedules are retained through game sessions, and when NPCs leave/join your party.
- Copying: Copy a survivors schedule to all others with a single button press.
- Automatic job switching: Every hour, survivors check what job they're supposed to be doing and their active task changes accordingly.
- Integrated into existing Remnants behavior and vision: If an NPC is not tired, he won't sleep-- likewise, if he is tired during the day, he might go to bed despite being assigned to work. I intended to maintain Pat's vision of having NPCs be more than just robots who do exactly as you say.

**Planned Features**:
- Creatable/swappable presets
- Integration into Remnants UI (rather than being a seperate menu)
- Improved access to job-specific settings per NPC when editing schedules.
- Better default schedule

## Planned Modules
### Automations & Improvements
A handful of minor features making Safehouse life a little safer, more enjoyable, or automated. These will be added over time, and not as a dedicated module.

**Planned Features**:
- Automatic stove shutoff

### Dynamic Guard Posts
Instead of guard posts being assigned to only one NPC, guard posts are created as shared locations that are handed out on a first-come, first-serve basis with priority and scheduling considerations. 

*Planned for the future... ;)*

**Planned Features**:
- Shared guard posts: Guard posts come from a shared pool, meaning you do not need to manually give every NPC a guard post
- Priority system: Guard posts will have a priority-- higher priority guard posts are reserved first before lower priority ones are taken.
- Scheduled hourly: Every hour, all guard posts are checked to see if a higher priority guard post should be manned. Two NPCs never guard the same guard post.

### Wandering Traders
Adds wandering traders that will periodically visit your safehouse every 2-5 days. They come with randomized inventories, and have a bartering system inspired by This War of Mine.

*Coming after Dynamic Guard Posts*

**Planned Features**:
- Randomly visiting traders: Traders will come randomly, but always at least once per week, and never before the 3rd day.
- Generated inventories: Traders will have randomly generated inventories, and weights will be assigned to those items semi-randomly.
- Bartering system: This War of Mine style bartering system, where there is no currency and no "point" system displayed on the screen.
